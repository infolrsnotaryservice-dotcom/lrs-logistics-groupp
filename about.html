About Us

A COMPANY BUILT ON SOLUTIONS.

Professional freight dispatching support, rooted in Richmond, Virginia and focused on helping carriers succeed.

Who We Are

YOUR PARTNER IN EVERY MILE.

LRS Logistics Group is a Richmond, Virginia-based freight dispatching company supporting independent owner-operators and fleet owners. We focus on clear communication, organized paperwork, and efficient coordination between carriers and freight brokers.

Our approach is service-driven: understanding your equipment, available hours, preferred lanes, and business goals before discussing load options.

In Loving Memory

HONORING THE LEGACY OF LEON R. STEWART

LRS Logistics Group was founded in loving memory of Leon Roosevelt Stewart, who passed away in December 2021. His name represents more than just the foundation of our company; it represents a legacy that will continue to live on through everything we build and accomplish.

LRS Logistics Group was created with purpose, determination, and a commitment to excellence. Every milestone, every partnership, and every success is another way of keeping his memory alive.

His legacy lives on. His name continues. His memory will never fade.
