About LRS Logistics Group

A DISPATCHING COMPANY BUILT ON SOLUTIONS.

LRS Logistics Group is a Virginia-based independent freight dispatching company providing dependable, professional, and efficient dispatching services for owner-operators and fleet owners. We connect carriers with freight opportunities, help negotiate rates, coordinate loads, and handle key paperwork so carriers can stay focused on the road.

Founded in loving memory of Leon Roosevelt Stewart, who passed away in December 2021, our company carries forward a legacy of dedication, hard work, and service.

What does a freight dispatcher do?

We help independent carriers locate potential loads, communicate with brokers, negotiate rates on the carrier's behalf as authorized, organize documents, and coordinate load details. Carriers make the final decisions about the loads they accept.

What types of trucks do you work with?

We welcome inquiries for dry vans, reefers, flatbeds, step decks, power-only, box trucks, hotshots, and other equipment types. Available opportunities depend on your equipment, authority, region, and market conditions.

How much does dispatching cost?

Pricing and service terms are discussed during your consultation and confirmed in a written dispatch agreement before services begin. Submitting an inquiry does not create a payment obligation.

Do I need an MC or USDOT number?

Please provide your USDOT and, if applicable, MC number so we can review your operating authority and service needs. Requirements may differ based on your operation and equipment type.

Will I choose which loads to accept?

Yes. We present options and coordinate communications, but you retain control over your business and whether to accept a load.

Does completing the carrier form mean I've signed up?

No. The form is only an inquiry. We will follow up using your contact information to discuss next steps. Dispatch services begin only after the appropriate written agreement is completed.

Do you guarantee loads or earnings?

No. Freight availability, rates, and earnings vary with market conditions, location, equipment, and other factors. We focus on responsive support and clear communication.
