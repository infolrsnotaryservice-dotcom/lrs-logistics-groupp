HOW TO PUBLISH
Upload the full contents of this ZIP, including the assets folder, to Netlify or another static web host. For Netlify Drop, extract this ZIP and drag the resulting site folder to Netlify. Keep the relative file structure intact.

FORMS
All three forms are configured for the previously supplied Formspree endpoint: https://formspree.io/f/meaolnvj
Each form includes an inquiry_type and _subject so you can distinguish career, carrier, and contact submissions in Formspree. Forms redirect to a local thank-you page only after Formspree responds successfully; on errors, they display an error and preserve the form.

IMPORTANT EMAIL SETUP
Sign into your Formspree dashboard and verify that this form is active, that your intended destination is info@lrslogisticsgroup.com, that the destination email is verified, and that email notifications are enabled. Send a real test from EACH form on the PUBLISHED site and confirm that the messages reach the inbox (and check spam). Formspree settings control the email delivery; changing the HTML alone cannot verify or guarantee it.
