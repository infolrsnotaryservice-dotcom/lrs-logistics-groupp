BUILT ON PURPOSE AND PROFESSIONALISM.

Hi, I’m Toniyah Garcia, the proud founder and owner of LRS Logistics Group. As a serial entrepreneur with multiple business ventures, I’ve always had a passion for building businesses, creating opportunities, and helping others succeed.

I started LRS Logistics Group with a vision of providing dependable, professional freight dispatching services while building lasting relationships with carriers. This business also holds a special place in my heart, as it was created to honor the memory of my grandfather, Leon Roosevelt Stewart.

My goal is to continue building a company rooted in integrity, dedication, and excellence. I believe that with hard work, determination, and faith, all things are possible.
